## 3. The eventual canonical licence text

How the finished licence text will itself be licensed is **an open decision**, deliberately
not pre-empted here. The practice in this licence family is a permissive documentation
licence that allows verbatim reuse with attribution while the licence *name* stays a
trademark conditioned on shipping the text unchanged — the mechanism that keeps one canonical
text canonical. That combination is the design intent; the exact instrument is for counsel
and the steward organization to settle when both exist.
